Create a landscape 3D modeling reference sheet for three harmless decorative tubular modules used as scenery in a colorful toy-like television game show videogame. Style reference attached: copy only the transparent blue-tinted plastic tubes with thick gold collars. No people, no machines, no actual functional equipment instructions. Off-white background, soft even light, simple rounded geometry. Three clearly separated horizontal groups: TUBO RECTO, CODO DE 90 GRADOS, ARO CONECTOR. For each group show six labeled visual views FRENTE, ESPALDA, IZQUIERDA, DERECHA, SUPERIOR, INFERIOR; front and rear of the straight section show the circular hollow opening, sides show a simple straight tube. The elbow shows its two hollow ends in the appropriate views. The connector is a simple thick gold ring with a visible central opening. Add a small 3/4 arrangement and separated decorative gold collar rings. Entire objects visible, no cropping, no numeric dimensions, no text beyond labels. Match the cheerful blue-gold rounded aesthetic of reference. Individual decorative prop modeling study only.
