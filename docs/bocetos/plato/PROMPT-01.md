# Prompt de generación — Concepto 01

Herramienta: generación de imágenes integrada; sin referencias y fondo opaco.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: first visual concept sheet for a cooperative 3D trivia videogame, exploratory concept rather than final assets.
Create a beautiful cohesive landscape concept sheet with three clear panels: a large main panel showing the entire game show studio; two smaller panels below showing the base player character front and side, and a detailed answer booth plus money props. High quality stylized 3D game art, rounded vinyl toy proportions with large heads and small bodies, original character designs, attractive softly lit colors and clean readable modular forms. Playful cheap television game show in fiction, but polished appealing rendering.
Main studio: exactly FOUR answer booths arranged in a semicircle labeled only A, B, C, D, each with an accessible front money tray and a separate standing space for a voluntarily wagered player. Each booth connects to a comically oversized curved pneumatic ejection tube going upward, no floor trapdoors. A central table with bundled banknotes and a simple manual exchange station. A separate big physical button beside the central table labeled "VERIFICAR". A screen above with a simple "PREGUNTA 1 / 15" and no detailed question text. A theatrical stylized host near the screen, with a small text speech bubble. Two original cute vinyl-proportioned contestants, one carrying a single banknote bundle, another standing near a booth. Give usable clear walkways, simple compact geometry appropriate for a small indie project. No casinos, roulette, weapons or complex laboratory machinery.
Character panel: same base contestant, large rounded head, simple expressive eyes, small rounded body, simple neutral jumpsuit that can support different outfits, mitten-like hands capable of holding money, front and side view, no brand logos, not a collectible product package.
Prop/booth panel: show one answer booth with money tray and separate player standing space, tube above, and three denomination props alongside: a banknote bundle, a cloth money bag, a gold bar. Do not put numerical denominations on them.
Composition: readable concept board with generous margins, main studio fills upper two-thirds, small lower character and booth studies. Minimal Spanish captions "PLATO", "PERSONAJE BASE", "CABINA Y DINERO". No HUD countdown clock, no time limit imagery, no extra answer booths, no cars or carts, no watermarks. This is an aesthetic proposal not a finalized level blueprint.
```
